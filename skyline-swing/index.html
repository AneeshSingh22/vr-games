SKYLINE SWING

On your Quest: open this page in the Meta Quest Browser, press ENTER VR, and step off the roof.

Computer: click to play · mouse aims · left-click = right web · right-click = left web (hold to swing, release to let go) · Shift = climb the web · WASD steer · Space = jump/flip · F = slow-mo · R = back to the roof
Phone: hold to swing, drag to look.
